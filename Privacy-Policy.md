Welcome to FIZI – Fitness and Workouts (“we”, “our”, or “us”).

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Personal Information

We may collect the following information if you choose to provide it:

- Name
- Email address

This data is used for account management, authentication, and important communication.

### b. Usage & Device Information

We may collect limited, non-personal information such as:

- Device model
- Operating system version
- App usage statistics

This data helps improve app performance and user experience.

### c. Camera & Physical Activity Data

FIZI uses your device’s camera to analyze workout movements for AI-based form guidance.

- **Local Processing Only**: All camera data is processed entirely on your device.
- **No Storage**: We do not record, store, or upload any photos or videos.
- **No Sharing**: Camera data is never shared with our servers or third parties.

Video frames are used temporarily for real-time pose estimation and immediately discarded.
